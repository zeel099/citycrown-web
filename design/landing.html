      How ranking works

      The biggest total ranks first. That's the whole rule.

      Each city and category pair is its own board. Rank is the sum of every bid on a listing, so a small board like Surat → Cafes can be topped for a few hundred rupees.

      1. Step 1

        Pick a board

        Choose a city and a category. Surat → Cafes is one board; Mumbai → Gyms is another.

      2. Step 2

        See the price to pass

        Every spot shows what it costs to move up: the total above you, minus yours, plus ₹10.

      3. Step 3

        Pay once

        Card, wallet or UPI, in rupees. Bids are final and add to the listing's total.

      4. Step 4

        The board re-ranks

        The new order appears as soon as the payment is confirmed, and a receipt goes to your email.
